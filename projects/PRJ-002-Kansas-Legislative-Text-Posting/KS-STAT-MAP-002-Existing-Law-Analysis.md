## 2. Statutory loci (SPEC-003 §6.1)

### 2.1 K.S.A. 75-4317a — “meeting” defined (KOMA)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Existing law (located) | Defines “meeting” for the open meetings act as any gathering or assembly in person or through the use of a telephone or any other medium for interactive communication by a majority of the membership of a public body or agency subject to this act for the purpose of discussing the business or affairs of the public body or agency |
| Source | [K.S.A. 75-4317a (Kansas Revisor)](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch75/075_043_0017a.html) — “meeting” definition confirmed against Revisor text 2026-11-06 |
| Likely later action (SPEC-003) | Amend to add repository safe harbor — **not drafted here** |
| Open questions | Exact safe-harbor wording; interaction with serial-communication rules in 75-4318(f); risk that a safe harbor could be misread as permitting deliberation without notice (SPEC-003 R-006) |

### 2.2 K.S.A. 75-4318 — open meetings coverage, notice, serial communications

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Existing law (located) | (a) Open-meeting coverage for legislative and administrative bodies and agencies of the state and political and taxing subdivisions, with exceptions. (b) Notice to persons requesting notice. (f) Interactive communications in a series treated as open under stated conditions. (g)(4) Open meetings law does not apply if otherwise provided by state or federal law **or by rules of the Kansas senate or house of representatives** |
| Source | [K.S.A. 75-4318 (Kansas Revisor)](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch75/075_043_0018.html); [KLRD KOMA summary](https://klrd.gov/kansas-open-meetings-act/) |
| SPEC-003 note | KLRD states the Legislature and its committees are covered unless rules provide otherwise — consistent with 75-4318(g)(4) |
| Likely later action | Conforming analysis only in Phase 1; no text drafted |
| Open questions | Whether chamber rules currently displace KOMA for any posting-relevant step `[TO BE VERIFIED]`; whether repository posting should be framed inside or outside KOMA “meeting” concepts |
