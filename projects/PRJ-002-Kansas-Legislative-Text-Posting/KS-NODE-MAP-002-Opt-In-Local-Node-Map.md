Maps the classes of local governing bodies that SPEC-003 contemplates for later **opt-in** adoption of posting rules. Exact enabling statute for opt-in remains `[TO BE VERIFIED]` (`KS-STAT-MAP-002` §2.4).

---

## 1. Design posture

1. Local adoption is **opt-in**, not mandatory annexation of local authority (SPEC-003 §10).  
2. Node classes below are identification classes for engineering, not a roster of named governments.  
3. No contributor or official personal information is stored.
