# Invite a Friend to PRJ-002

Anyone may use these messages to invite someone to help draft the Kansas One-Hour Posting Rule.
Copy one, change `[Name]`, and send it by Messenger, text or email. Put it in your own words if you like.

Inviting someone grants them nothing. Everyone follows the same path in [ONBOARDING.md](ONBOARDING.md),
and only the Human Engineer records a level (SPEC-003 §18).

---

## Option 1 — Personal ask (everything in one message)

```text
Hey [Name], you know how we never get to see what's in a bill until it's already passed? I'm helping do something about it.

A small group is drafting a Kansas rule: no vote on any bill until its exact text has been public for at least one hour. Nonpartisan. The draft is due November 30, and I'd like you in on it.

You don't need to be a lawyer or a programmer. You'll make a free GitHub account, read five short lessons (about 3 minutes each), and take a 5-minute check. After that you review the draft with us.

Start here:
https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Legislative-Manager/blob/main/ONBOARDING.md

If you use Claude, paste this in and it'll walk you through it:
"Read https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Legislative-Manager/blob/main/ONBOARDING.md. I'm new. Tell me where I am and what my next step is, one step at a time."

Want in?
```

## Option 2 — Short ask first (send the link after they say yes)

```text
Hey [Name], quick question. Would you help draft a Kansas rule that says no bill gets voted on until its exact text has been public for at least an hour? Nonpartisan, free, about 20 minutes to get started. If you're in, I'll send you the link.
```

When they say yes, send them the onboarding link and the Claude prompt from Option 1.

---

## Before you send

- **Don't post anyone's contact details in GitHub.** Not theirs, not yours.
- **Don't promise a role.** Every newcomer starts at Step 1 of [ONBOARDING.md](ONBOARDING.md).
- **If they hold public office,** tell them their comments about official business may be public records,
  and that discussion among a majority of their own public body belongs in a noticed meeting (SPEC-003 §18.3(1)).
- **Keep it nonpartisan.** PRJ-002 argues for a process, not for any party's bills.
